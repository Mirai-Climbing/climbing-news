## Outdoor Ascents

**Anon Matsufuji climbed The Swarm, a V13/14 boulder in the Buttermilks, California, in four tries** (November 7)

Gripped reports that it is her second-hardest boulder send, after her V14 ascent of Utsushiyo in Japan earlier this year.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/buttermilks-classic-the-swarm-v13-14-topped-by-anon-matsufuji/)

**Wang Bingtang became the first Chinese climber to redpoint a 5.15** (November 7)

The 18-year-old member of the Chongqing Climbing Team sent El Gran Cabrón (5.15b) at Shegeng Cave in Guangxi, a route first climbed by Seb Bouin in 2025.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/wang-bingtang-is-the-first-chinese-climber-to-send-5-15/)

**Adam Ondra established Scettro, a V14/15 boulder in Val Daone, Italy** (November 7)

The new problem links into Il Trono (V13), which Ondra flashed in July, according to Gripped.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/adam-ondra-establishes-v14-15-in-val-daone/)

**Ryuichi Murai climbed Alphane, a V17 boulder in Switzerland** (November 6)

It is his second V17 of the year, after Return of the Sleepwalker. Shawn Raboutou first climbed Alphane in 2022.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/ryuichi-murai-climbs-another-v17-with-alphane/)

## Gyms

**A new climbing gym with more than 12,000 square feet of wall is being built in Prattville, Alabama** (November 5)

The Hanger is still under construction, and the article gives no opening date. Maverick Climbing is building the walls.
