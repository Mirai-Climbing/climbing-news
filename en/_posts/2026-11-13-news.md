## Crags & Access

**Access Fund bought the parking and trail access to Phun Rock in Washington** (November 6)

The land in the Methow Valley used to belong to Outward Bound. Access Fund says it plans to hand the parcel to the U.S. Forest Service, and it is asking climbers to donate toward the purchase and upkeep.

[Access Fund](https://accessfund.org/latest-news/washington-acquisition)

**Access Fund gave out $15,000 in anchor replacement grants** (November 9)

The total is up from $10,000 in 2025, with Arc'teryx as presenting partner. Three of five applications were fully funded, covering hardware replacement at crags in Utah, Kentucky, Colorado, and Wyoming, plus volunteer training for Boulder's anchor program.

[Access Fund](https://accessfund.org/latest-news/access-fund-awards-15000-in-anchor-replacement-grants)

## Outdoor Ascents

**Tyler Thompson became the fifth climber to finish Jumbo Love (5.15b) in California** (November 11)

The route is at Clark Mountain, and it is Thompson's first climb at this grade. Chris Sharma made the first ascent in 2008.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/tyler-thompson-climbs-jumbo-love-5-15b/)

**Adam Ondra made the first ascent of Huga Chaga, proposed at 5.15c, in Arco, Italy** (November 11)

He bolted the line in 2022 and started projecting it this autumn. It begins with a hard 5.14d section, then leads to a kneebar and a V14 section.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/adam-ondra-makes-first-ascent-of-huga-chaga-5-15c/)

## Gyms

**Touchstone announced Aisle 19, a bouldering gym in a former San Francisco supermarket** (November 11)

It is the chain's 19th location and is expected to open in early 2027. It has 27,764 square feet in total, with 10,645 square feet of climbing wall and Kilter, Tension, and Touchstone boards. It returns Touchstone to the city where its first gym opened in 1995.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/touchstone-builds-on-roots-expands-in-san-francisco/)
