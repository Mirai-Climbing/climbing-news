## Outdoor Ascents

**Hamish McArthur flashed Silent Menace (5.14c) in Squamish, Canada** (November 8)

The 24-year-old climbed the route at the Cacodemon boulders. Gripped says it is the first known flash of the line, which was first climbed by Sonnie Trotter and later renamed Smell the Glove after a hold broke.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/hamish-mcarthur-flashes-silent-menace-5-14c-in-squamish/)

**Two Italian climbers, Gio Placci and Filip Schenk, climbed Naturalmente (5.15a/9a+) in Camiore, Italy** (November 8)

The route at the San Rocchino sector was first climbed by Adam Ondra in 2017, and Gripped notes it took him three days.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/two-italians-send-an-adam-ondra-5-15a/)

## Gyms

**Nova Boulders, a bouldering and training gym in Burlingame, California, is expected to open in the coming weeks** (November 4)

The gym fills a 12,000-square-foot space, with about 3,500 square feet of climbing wall across training boards and bouldering walls. No exact opening date is given.
