## Outdoor Ascents

**American Katie Lamb climbed two V14 boulders in one session in Austria** (October 24)

Lamb sent Bügeleisen and Hide and Sick, both graded V14 (8B+), in the Maltatal. Bügeleisen is a crimpy, overhanging problem first climbed in 2001. The report was published this week, and it says the session took place in October 2025.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/katie-lamb-climbing-bugeleisen-v14/)

**Pietro Vidi made the second ascent of Drifter's Escape (5.15a) in Squamish** (October 23)

Vidi repeated Connor Herson's 2025 trad route on the Chief, confirming the proposed 5.15a grade. This makes it the first trad climb with a confirmed 5.15a grade. It took him 15 sessions, with 10 lead attempts.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/pietro-vidi-makes-second-ascent-of-drifters-escape-5-15a-trad-in-squamish/)

**Adam Ondra made the first ascent of Murena (5.14d) in Italy** (October 24)

Ondra climbed the Piedmont sport route, which was bolted by Matteo Gambaro in 2012, on October 16, the same day he sent the boulder Exodia.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/adam-ondra-makes-5-14d-first-ascent-in-italy/)

## Gyms

**Sender One Climbing and COROS announce a wearables partnership** (October 20)

The Southern California gym operator and the watch maker say Sender One coaches will give feedback on how COROS watches track climbing training and recovery. COROS is working on climbing-specific features such as route grades and climbing volume. This is a sponsor-written release, not the publication's own reporting.

**Guide to climbing at the 2028 Los Angeles Games** (October 23)

The guide says Olympic climbing will award separate medals for bouldering, lead, and speed for the first time, with 76 athletes. Para climbing makes its Paralympic debut with 80 athletes competing for eight medals. Olympic climbing runs in July 2028 and para climbing from August 24 to 27.
