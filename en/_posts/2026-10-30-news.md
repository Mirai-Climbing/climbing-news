## Outdoor Ascents

**Daniel Woods repeated Sword of Damocles (V16) on Mount Blue Sky, Colorado** (October 29)

Woods got the boulder, first climbed by Austin Purdy in 2024, this October after cold alpine conditions arrived with a snowfall. The road to the area closes to cars in October, so he used an e-bike for part of the approach.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/daniel-woods-sends-sword-of-damocles-v16/)

**Shawn Raboutou made the first ascent of Chau Putos (V14/15) in El Chaltén, Argentina** (October 29)

The send was made on a trip in January and was shared on Instagram, according to the article. The problem includes a throw to a lip before the top-out.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/shawn-raboutou-makes-v14-15-first-ascent-in-patagonia/)

**Three American alpinists made the first ski and snowboard descents of the south face of Dorje Lhakpa in Nepal** (October 28)

Nick Russell, Adam Fabrikant and Billy Haas reached the 6,966 m summit on October 20 after about 7.5 hours of alpine-style climbing, then came down in under two hours.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/americans-make-himalayan-history-with-first-descent/)

**Leo Houlding's team climbed a new 750 m big wall, Chossrider, on Baffin Island** (October 29)

Houlding and Eric Boomer finished the 21-pitch route (5.10+ X M5 R A3+) on The Beak in May 2026, over 10 days with five nights on portaledges. Winds reached about 60 mph.
