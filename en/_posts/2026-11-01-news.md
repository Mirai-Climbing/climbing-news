## Outdoor Ascents

**Oriane Bertone made the first female ascent of The Big Island (V15) in Fontainebleau, France** (October 31)

The send came a week after she won the Boulder World Cup in Salt Lake City. She worked the compression boulder for about a month between competitions and sent it on her first try of the fifth session. She said she has no immediate plans for Soudain Seul, a V17 sit start on the same boulder.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/oriane-bertone-makes-first-female-ascent-of-the-big-island-v15/)

**Jessica Pilz redpointed Kraftplatzl (5.14d) in Innsbruck, Austria, in one session** (October 31)

She climbed it on October 29, about two weeks after sending Hades, also 5.14d, in the same area. The short, bouldery route was first climbed by David Lama in 2015.
