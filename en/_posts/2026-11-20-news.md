## Gyms

**Movement, the US gym chain, has a new CEO** (November 19)

Ryan Sewell, who has worked at Movement for about 17 years and most recently led experience and routesetting, takes over as chief executive. The previous CEO, Anne-Worley Moelter, moves to chair the board. Movement runs 34 open gyms across the US.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/movement-appoints-ryan-sewell-as-new-ceo/)

## Outdoor Ascents

**Alex Megos made the second ascent of Artaburu (5.15b) in Margalef, Spain** (November 19)

Iker Pou first climbed the very steep, roof-heavy route in 2018, and it stayed unrepeated for about eight years. Megos did it on a short trip tied to the Siurana Climbing Festival. It is his third 5.15b of 2026.
