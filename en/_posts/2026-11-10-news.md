## Competitions

**Team USA collected 13 medals at the World Climbing Para Series finale in Fukuoka, Japan** (November 9)

The season-ending event ran November 5–6. USA Climbing lists four golds, including Linn Poston, Wanying Morningstar, Brian Zarzuela and Ethan Zilz, plus silvers and bronzes across several categories.

**Team USA took third place in both boulder and lead at the Nations Grand Finale in Fukuoka** (November 9)

On November 7–8, the US team scored 154 points in boulder and 105 in lead, finishing ahead of Switzerland's 96.5 in lead. Japan won both competitions, with South Korea second in each.
