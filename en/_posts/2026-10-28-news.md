## Outdoor Ascents

**Nicolai and Timo Užnik climbed the V15 boulder Defying Gravity in Colorado** (October 27)

Daniel Woods first climbed the problem in 2013. Nicolai sent it in about an hour, and his brother Timo also finished it, which was Timo's second V15. The report calls the line one of the best boulders in the United States.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/uznik-brothers-send-maybe-the-best-boulder-in-the-world-in-colorado/)

**Nigel Armino of the Netherlands climbed Action Directe (5.14d) in Germany** (October 27)

Wolfgang Güllich opened the Frankenjura route in 1991 as the world's first 5.14d. Armino tore a finger pulley on it in 2023, came back in 2025, and became the first Dutch climber to send it, using the newer, shorter sequence.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/nigel-armino-climbs-the-iconic-action-directe-5-14d/)

**Two climbers were rescued after about 18 hours on Mount Andromeda in the Canadian Rockies** (October 27)

According to the report, the pair fell on The Andromeda Strain in early October and were stranded high on the route. Parks Canada rescuers were flown in, climbed to the top and lowered them with a 100-meter rope rescue before helping them down. It is the second rescue from a classic Rockies alpine route this season.
