## Crags & Access

**Access Fund bought the parking area and trail that serve Phun Rock in Washington** (November 6)

The crag in the Methow Valley is on Forest Service land, but the practical access ran across private land. Access Fund plans to hand the parking area to the Forest Service later and is asking for donations to cover costs and upkeep until then. The group says it has now acquired or protected close to 100 crags nationwide.

[Access Fund](https://www.accessfund.org/latest-news/washington-acquisition)

**Access Fund gave $15,000 in anchor replacement grants to five local climbing groups** (November 9)

The total is up from $10,000 last year. The projects are in Utah, Kentucky, Colorado and Wyoming, and each group also gets a rope access kit. Examples include replacing about 200 bolts at Rotary Park in Ouray and new hardware at Oil Crack and Arena in the Red River Gorge.

[Access Fund](https://www.accessfund.org/latest-news/access-fund-awards-15000-in-anchor-replacement-grants)
