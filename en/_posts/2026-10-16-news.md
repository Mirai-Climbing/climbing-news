## Competitions

**Salt Lake City hosts the last Boulder event of the World Climbing Series** (October 15)

The sixth and final boulder stop runs October 16–18. Sorato Anraku of Japan has won all five earlier boulder events, and a sixth win would match what Janja Garnbret did in 2019. In the women's race, Erin McNeice, Oceania Mackenzie and Annie Sanders are all still chasing a first series title.

## Gyms

**New gym planned for Twisp, Washington** (October 14)

Methow Spires is expected to open in November with 1,920 sq ft of roped climbing and over 1,200 sq ft of bouldering. It is owned by a local couple.

**Boulder, Colorado tops a ranking of US indoor climbing markets** (October 16)

A new index compares commercial climbing to market size, using gym data from April 2026. Boulder scores 275.9, ahead of the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont area (49.2) and Boston-Cambridge-Newton (35.4). New York City has the most gyms (37) and over 450,000 sq ft of climbing.
