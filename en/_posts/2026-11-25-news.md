## Outdoor Ascents

**Japan's Ritsu Kayotani climbed Return of the Sleepwalker at Red Rock, Nevada, and calls it V17** (November 24)

It is his second V17 after a send in Colorado last month. The problem is a sit start to Sleepwalker, and some climbers who have done it think it is softer than V17.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/without-a-doubt-the-greatest-moment-of-my-life-ritsu-kayotani-on-latest-v17-send/)

**Alex Megos made the second ascent of Artaburu (5.15b) in Margalef, Spain** (reported November 24)

Iker Pou first climbed the very steep roof route in 2018. Megos sent it on November 5 after a few days of trying, and it was his third 5.15b of 2026.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/one-of-the-craziest-roof-sequences-ive-ever-seen-on-a-route-alex-megos-climbing-5-15b/)

**Ryuichi Murai repeated Return of the Dreamtime (V16) in Ticino, Switzerland** (reported November 23)

On the same month-long trip he also climbed Alphane (V17). He says Return of the Dreamtime may be closer to V15, though he thinks it is about a grade harder than Dreamtime.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/ryuichi-murai-climbs-return-of-the-dreamtime-v16/)
