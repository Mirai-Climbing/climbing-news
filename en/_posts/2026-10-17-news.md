## Crags & Access

**Access Fund hands out $30,484 in conservation grants to eight groups** (October 13)

Projects include a new approach trail near Flagstaff, wider flood-resistant bridges in Red River Gorge, trail repairs in New River Gorge National Park, and a cliff ecology conference at Acadia. The program has now supported 518 projects since 1991.

[Access Fund](https://www.accessfund.org/latest-news/access-fund-grants-30000-to-support-climbing-area-conservation)

## Outdoor Ascents

**Owen Whaley sends Creature From the Black Lagoon, V16, in Colorado** (October 16)

The 2016 Daniel Woods problem at Upper Chaos is Whaley's third V16. He made a point of topping it out all the way.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/owen-whaley-climbs-american-test-piece-problem/)

**Debate over the grade of Exodia in Italy** (October 16)

Elias Iagnemma proposed V18 for the boulder after 211 sessions over four and a half years. A report says Adam Ondra thinks the harder second half is closer to V15, which could pull the overall grade down.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/adam-ondra-tries-exodia-v18-and-other-boulder-news/)

**Antonia Rüede-Passul walks a record-high women's highline on Trango Tower** (October 16)

The 21-metre line, rigged at 6,251 metres, beats her own earlier mark of 5,860 metres. Lukas Irmler was also on the trip.
