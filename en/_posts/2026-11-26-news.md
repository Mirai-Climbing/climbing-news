## Gyms

**Workers at a Movement gym in Philadelphia held a one-day strike over pay and scheduling** (November 17)

The union says the company has not bargained in good faith and faces unfair labor practice charges. It also says over 2,000 members have pledged to freeze memberships or boycott if asked. This is a press release written by the union side, and the article does not include Movement's response.

## Gear

**Escape Climbing bought the brand and catalog of Binary Climbing Holds** (November 19)

Binary, a Calgary-based maker of thermoformed gym holds and shapes, will have its core lines made at Escape's factories in Minnesota. Existing orders will be filled as scheduled, and the catalog is expected to be orderable through Escape in the coming months. This is a sponsored press release.
