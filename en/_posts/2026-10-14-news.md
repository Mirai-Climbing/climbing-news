## Outdoor Ascents

**Jakob Schubert makes the second ascent of DNA (5.15d / 9c)** (October 10; reported October 13)

Schubert repeated Seb Bouin's 2022 route at La Ramirole in the Verdon Gorge, France. The attempt was streamed live on YouTube, which Gripped calls a historic moment for the sport.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/profiles/why-jakob-schuberts-latest-5-15d-ascent-is-a-historic-moment-in-climbing/)

**Mei Kotake sends her first V15 in Gottardo, Switzerland** (October 14)

The day after climbing two V14s, Kotake topped P.I.G., a boulder first climbed by Stefano Ghisolfi in July 2026.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/mei-kotake-climbs-her-first-v15/)

## Gyms

**A family-run gym is set to open in Washington's Methow Valley** (October 14)

Methow Spires in Twisp plans to open in November 2026, with about 1,920 sq ft of roped climbing, over 1,200 sq ft of bouldering, a Kilter Board and a strength area.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/family-owned-climbing-gym-opening-in-outdoorsy-area-of-northern-washington/)
