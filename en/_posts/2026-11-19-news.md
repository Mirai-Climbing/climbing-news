## Outdoor Ascents

**Sean Bailey made the first ascent of Odyssey (5.15a), the first 5.15 at Red River Gorge** (November 17)

The route on the Bronaugh Wall was bolted by Adam Taylor several years ago. Seb Bouin had tried it the year before and came close. Bailey describes it as a long line of 80 to 100 moves that ends with an all-points-off dyno.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/sean-bailey-climbs-new-5-15a-at-red-river-gorge/)

**Yannick Flohé climbed the V15 boulder Eye in the Sky in Ticino, Switzerland** (November 18)

It was his eighth V15 and his third attempt at a flash on one. He succeeded on his third try on the problem. Giuliano Cameroni first climbed it in 2024 and proposed V16, but a broken crimp lowered the grade to V15, which repeat climbers have agreed with.
