## Outdoor Ascents

**Four climbers repeated V15 boulder problems in the US, Japan and Switzerland** (November 22)

Ben Hanna and Landers Gaydosh climbed Doors of Perception in Little Cottonwood Canyon, Utah. Tomoa Narasaki sent Vanitas in Horai, Japan, Min Hyunbin sent Dreamtime in Ticino, and Yufei Pan sent Forgotten Gem in Ticino. All four were repeats, not first ascents.
