## Competitions

**Team USA collected nine medals at the World Climbing Para Series in Gunsan, South Korea** (November 2)

The American para team won five golds and four silvers. Gold went to Hassan Mohammadi (Men's B3), Melissa Ruiz (Women's RP1), Nat Vorel (Women's RP3), Brian Zarzuela (Men's AU2) and Nathan McKinley (Men's AU3).
