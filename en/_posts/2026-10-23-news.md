## Crags & Access

**Access Fund will share half of Joint Membership money with local groups** (October 19)

Starting November 2, half of the net Joint Membership contributions will go to participating local climbing organizations, replacing the old flat payments per gift. The minimum Joint Membership level also drops from $50 to $5.

[Access Fund](https://www.accessfund.org/latest-news/joint-membership-updates)

**Access Fund awards new conservation grants to eight advocacy groups** (October 13)

The grants total $30,484. Projects cover areas such as Acadia, Flagstaff, New River Gorge, Chattanooga, Red River Gorge, the southern Sierra Nevada and Wisconsin state parks.

[Access Fund](https://www.accessfund.org/latest-news/access-fund-grants-30000-to-support-climbing-area-conservation)

## Gyms

**A new climbing gym is set to open in Twisp, Washington** (October 14)

Methow Spires in the Methow Valley is expected to open in November 2026, with about 1,920 sq ft of roped climbing and over 1,200 sq ft of bouldering.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/family-owned-climbing-gym-opening-in-outdoorsy-area-of-northern-washington/)

## Events

**Guide collects facts on climbing at the 2028 Los Angeles Games** (October 23)

The industry guide notes that Para Climbing debuts at the Paralympics with 80 athletes competing for eight medals. Olympic climbing will award separate Boulder, Lead and Speed medals, with 76 athletes (38 men and 38 women).
