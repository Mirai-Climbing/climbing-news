## Competitions

**Team USA won 13 medals at the final Para Series event of the season in Fukuoka** (November 9)

The competition was held in Fukuoka, Japan, on November 5–6. USA Climbing reports 4 gold, 4 silver, and 5 bronze medals for the U.S. team, including golds for Linn Poston, Wanying Morningstar, Brian Zarzuela, and Ethan Zilz.

[USA Climbing](https://usaclimbing.org/news/team-usa-closes-out-world-climbing-para-series-season-with-13-medals-in-fukuoka/)

**Team USA took third place in both Boulder and Lead at the Nations Grand Finale** (November 9)

At the event in Fukuoka on November 7–8, the U.S. scored 154 points in Boulder and 105 in Lead, ahead of Switzerland's 96.5 in Lead. Japan won both disciplines, and South Korea was second in both.

[USA Climbing](https://usaclimbing.org/news/team-usa-earns-two-podium-finishes-at-nations-grand-finale/)

**USA Climbing set the dates for the 2027 Collegiate and Youth National Championships** (November 12)

Collegiate Nationals run May 20–23, 2027, with Lead and Speed at the National Training Center in Salt Lake City and Boulder at Momentum Fort Union in Midvale, Utah. Youth Nationals run July 24–31, 2027, at the National Training Center.
