## Outdoor Ascents

**Dayan Akhtar and Tyler Thompson logged big US sends** (November 15)

Gripped's weekly roundup says Akhtar climbed the V17 boulder Return of the Sleepwalker in Red Rock, Nevada, on November 3. It also says Thompson made the fifth ascent of Jumbo Love (5.15b) at Clark Mountain.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/5-15-slab-5-15c-v17-and-more-climbing-news/)

**Jacopo Larcher visited the Gunks and repeated hard trad routes** (November 15)

In New York, Larcher flashed Ozone (5.13d) and repeated Brozone (5.14a), both trad routes, according to the same roundup.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/5-15-slab-5-15c-v17-and-more-climbing-news/)

**Adam Ondra made the first ascent of Huga Chaga (5.15c) in Arco, Italy** (November 15)

He bolted the route in 2022 and began working it seriously this autumn. The roundup also reports a V17 repeat by Ryuichi Murai in Switzerland and a second ascent of the V17 Realm of Tor'ment in the UK by Matthew Mendes.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/5-15-slab-5-15c-v17-and-more-climbing-news/)

**Jessy Pilz sent Solitary Souls (5.14c) in two tries** (November 15)

The Austrian Olympic bronze medallist, mostly known as a competitor, has been focusing on outdoor rock in 2026. She also climbed Gravity Games (5.13d/5.14a) on her second go.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/jessy-pilz-climbs-5-14c-on-her-second-go/)

**Sergio San Martín Fernandez climbed his first 5.15b in Spain** (November 14)

La Planta de Shiva in Villanueva del Rosario took him 18 days over two trips. He started climbing in 2020 and has added several 5.14d and 5.15a routes this season.
