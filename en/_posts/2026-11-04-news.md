## Competitions

**USA Climbing published its 2027 national championship and team trials schedule** (November 3)

Nationals are planned for Austin, Texas in early February, Para Nationals for Cypress, Texas in late February, and the National Team Trials in the Los Angeles area in mid-March. Collegiate and youth dates will come later.

[USA Climbing](https://usaclimbing.org/news/usa-climbing-announces-2027-national-championships-and-team-trials-schedule/)

## Outdoor Ascents

**Daniel Woods climbed Gateway, a V14 boulder problem in Colorado** (November 2)

The problem was set up by Drew Ruana in 2023 and first proposed at V15. Woods's ascent is reported at V14.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/daniel-woods-climbs-a-drew-ruana-v14-in-colorado/)

**Willow Petrobelli sent Víctimes del Futur in Margalef, Spain, her first 5.14c/d** (November 3)

The route was first climbed in 2006, and climbers still disagree on whether it is 5.14c or 5.14d. She also climbed Enara (5.14b) two days later.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/willow-petrobelli-climbs-her-first-5-14c-d/)

**Matthew Mendes made the second ascent of Realm of Tor'ment, his first V17** (November 3)

The 20-year-old from the UK repeated Will Bosi's Peak District problem (V17 / 9A).
