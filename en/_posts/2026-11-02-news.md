## Outdoor Ascents

**Laura Rogora climbed Tecnoking (5.14d) in Chile on her second try** (November 1)

The send came a few days after her silver medal at the Lead World Cup in Santiago. The route is at Las Chilcas, a crag with fragile rock near the Pan-American Highway. The article also notes her 2026 ascent of Bibliographie (5.15c) at Céüse, France, in six days.

**Claudia Ghisolfi sent Alone (5.14d) and Abyss (5.14c) in Gorges du Loup, France** (November 1)

Alone, first climbed by Pierre le Cerf in 2021, was her third 5.14d according to her 8a.nu profile. Abyss was established in 2006, and climbers disagree about its grade; she graded it 5.14c.
