## Crags & Access

**Access Fund changes how Joint Membership money reaches local groups** (October 19)

From November 2, half of net Joint Membership contributions will go to the local climbing organizations taking part. Joint Membership will be open from $5, and members giving $75 or more a year get an Access Fund t-shirt.

[Access Fund](https://accessfund.org/latest-news/joint-membership-updates)

## Outdoor Ascents

**Adam Ondra repeats Exodia, the boulder proposed as the first V18** (October 18)

Ondra finished the Italian boulder in four days, using the sequence of first ascensionist Elias Iagnemma. He feels it is closer to V17 but respects the V18 proposal, saying the first true V18 is still to come.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/adam-ondra-climbs-exodia-the-worlds-first-proposed-v18/)

**Lara Neumeier makes the first female ascent of Rhapsody (E11, 5.14c R)** (October 7)

The German climber topped the Scottish trad route after 16 days of work over two trips. Dave MacLeod first climbed Rhapsody in 2006, so her ascent came 20 years later.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/lara-neumeier-makes-historic-first-female-ascent-of-rhapsody-e11/)
