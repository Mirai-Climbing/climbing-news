## Competitions

**USA Climbing published its 2027 national event calendar** (November 3)

The YETI Climbing National Championships are set for February 3–7, 2027, in Austin, Texas. Para Nationals follow on February 27–28 in Cypress, Texas, and National Team Trials run March 10–14 in the Los Angeles area. Collegiate and Youth dates are still to come.

[USA Climbing](https://usaclimbing.org/yeti-national-championships/)

## Outdoor Ascents

**British climber Dayan Akhtar sent a V17 in Red Rock, Nevada** (November 3)

Akhtar, 22, climbed Return of the Sleepwalker (V17), a sit-start version of Sleepwalker, in six sessions. It is his first V17.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/british-climber-dayan-akhtar-sends-a-nevada-v17/)

**Luca Bana made the first ascent of Grandmaster (5.15b) in Italy** (October 24)

The new route adds a lower start of about 10 metres to his earlier line The Master. Bana did it on his second try that day and suggested the 5.15b grade himself.
