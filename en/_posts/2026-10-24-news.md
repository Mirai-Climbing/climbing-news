## Gyms

**Boulder, Colorado ranks first among US metro areas for climbing gym density** (October 16)

A new market ranking lists ten US metro areas with the most commercial climbing relative to population and land area. Boulder (275.9) leads, followed by San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont (49.2) and Boston-Cambridge-Newton (35.4). The report counts nearly 700 US commercial gyms at the start of 2026.
