## Outdoor Ascents

**Connor Herson freed two El Capitan routes, The Prophet (5.13d/5.14aR) and Freerider (5.13a), in a single day** (November 20)

Herson started The Prophet and flashed its crux pitch, but fell on the last pitch, so the route was not a full flash. He then climbed Freerider that afternoon, finishing about 18.5 hours after he began. Alex Honnold and Tommy Caldwell supported him on the two routes. Only Caldwell had done two El Capitan free routes in a day before (2005).
