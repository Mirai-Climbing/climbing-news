## Outdoor Ascents

**Four competition climbers repeated Sleepwalker in Red Rock, Nevada, and all suggested V15** (October 31)

Dayan Akhtar, Yuta Imaizumi, Nikolay Rusev and Raffael Gruber each climbed the boulder problem, which Jimmy Webb first climbed in 2018 at V16. The article says all four gave it V15 after their ascents.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/four-climbers-send-sleepwalker-all-call-it-v15/)

**Makoto Yamauchi climbed Burden of Dreams (V17) in Lappnor, Finland** (October 30)

It was his first V17 and the problem's sixth ascent. He came close in October 2025, paused for about six months, and returned in spring 2026 for a livestreamed send. The article adds that Noah Wheeler repeated it about a month later.
