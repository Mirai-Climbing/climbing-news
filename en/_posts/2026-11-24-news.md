## Gyms

**Movement, the largest US climbing gym chain, has a new CEO** (November 19)

The company promoted longtime employee and routesetter Ryan Sewell to chief executive. He takes over from Anne-Worley Moelter, who moves to the role of Chairwoman of the Board.

## Gear

**Escape Climbing bought the Canadian hold maker Binary Climbing Holds** (November 19)

Binary, based in Calgary, made vacuum-formed plastic holds and macros. Minnesota-based Escape will add its shapes to its own line, move core production to its facilities, and honor existing orders.
