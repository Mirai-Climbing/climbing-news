**Team USA won medals in eight categories at the World Climbing Para Series in Gunsan** (November 2)

## Crags & Access

**Access Fund bought the parking area that serves Phun Rock in Washington** (November 6)

The crag, with more than 160 routes in the Methow Valley, is on national forest land, but the main parking and trail crossed private property. Access Fund purchased that parcel and plans to hand it to the U.S. Forest Service for long-term ownership. It is asking for donations to cover the cost.
