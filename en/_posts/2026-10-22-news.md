## Outdoor Ascents

**Noah Wheeler repeats Burden of Dreams, V17** (October 20)

Wheeler, a pro climber from the US, needed seven sessions over two trips this spring. He is said to be the seventh person to repeat the problem that Nalle Hukkataival first climbed in 2016, and it is his third V17.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/noah-wheeler-climbs-burden-of-dreams-v17/)

**Jana Švecová climbs L'Oppio dei Climbers, V14 (8B+), in Italy** (October 21)

The boulder in Val Daone is described as finicky, with both physical and technical difficulty. She sent it soon after Katie Lamb's ascent.

## Gyms

**Tulsa's only bouldering gym is listed for sale** (October 20)

Gravity Bear in downtown Tulsa, Oklahoma, is looking for a new operator. The listing, a sponsor-written press release, mentions about 8,000 sq ft with 3,200 sq ft of climbing wall, a Tension Board 2, a fitness area and a café.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/gravity-bear-tulsas-only-bouldering-gym-is-for-sale/)

**A new bouldering gym opens in Marginea, northern Romania** (October 21)

The gym opened in August 2026 in the Bucovina region. It covers 325 square meters in total, with about 80 square meters of climbing wall up to 4.4 meters high.

[Climbing Business Journal](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/bouldering-hub-opens-in-northern-romania-centered-on-a-shared-experience/)
