## Competitions

**Yonatan Katz of Israel wins the men's boulder final in Salt Lake City** (October 18)

Katz topped the last boulder on his fourth attempt to finish on 69.7 points, ahead of Max Milne of Great Britain (59.7) and Rei Kawamata of Japan (54.2). It was only his second senior World Climbing Series start. Sorato Anraku of Japan had already secured the men's Boulder series title.

[World Climbing](https://www.worldclimbing.com/events/world-climbing-series-salt-lake-city-2026/news/katz-gets-gold-on-only-second-senior-start)

**Oriane Bertone wins the women's final as Erin McNeice takes the Boulder series** (October 19)

The French climber won with 84.6 points, with McNeice (69.7) second and Mao Nakamura of Japan (69.6) third. McNeice finished first in the season standings on 4545 points, ahead of Oce Mackenzie (4080) and Annie Sanders of the USA (3755). Lead and Speed now move to Santiago, Chile.
