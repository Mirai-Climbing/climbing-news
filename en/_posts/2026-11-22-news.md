## Outdoor Ascents

**Stefano Ghisolfi climbed three 5.15b routes in Spain while chasing four** (November 21)

Ghisolfi, from Italy, took on a goal of four 5.15b routes. He finished The Full Journey (Margalef, third ascent), Sleeping Lion (Siurana, fourth ascent) and Neanderthal (Santa Linya, fourth ascent). He left Fight or Flight in Oliana unfinished because of warm weather.
