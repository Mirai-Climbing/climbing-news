## Outdoor Ascents

**Cy McIntosh made the first ascent of Keep Feeding Ya (5.14d) near Helena, Montana** (October 26)

The 20-year-old climbed a roughly 60-meter roof to a blue streak, after projecting it every weekend for two months. He then bolted a longer extension up the streak, adding more than 15 meters of hard climbing above the 5.14d; the article gives no grade for that extension.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/new-5-14d-climbed-in-montana-by-cy-mcintosh/)

**Czech competition climber Martin Stráník made the first ascent of Problém (5.14d) in Adršpach** (October 28)

The sandstone arete on the Karlik tower was one of the area's oldest projects. Stráník needed 10 sessions, and the article says it may be slightly harder than the area's earlier 5.14d, To Tu Ještě Nebylo.
