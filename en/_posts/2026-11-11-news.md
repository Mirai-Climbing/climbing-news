**Touchstone plans Aisle 19, a bouldering gym in a former San Francisco grocery store** (November 11)

It will be the company's 19th location, with about 10,645 square feet of climbing surface and walls up to 16 feet tall. Boards, fitness and yoga space, and a sauna are planned, with an opening expected in early 2027.
