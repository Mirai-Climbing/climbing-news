## Competitions

**Team USA took five golds and four silvers at the Para Series lead event in Gunsan, South Korea** (November 2)

The event was held in late October. Gold went to Melissa Ruiz, Hassan Mohammadi, Nat Vorel, Brian Zarzuela (won on countback against Norway's Isak Ripman) and Nathan McKinley. Silver went to Gordon Fralick, Seneida Biendarra, Maureen Beck and Ethan Zilz.
