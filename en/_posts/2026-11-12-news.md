## Gyms

**Two Bay Area routesetters are about to open Nova Boulders, a bouldering gym in Burlingame, California** (November 4)

The gym has about 12,000 square feet of space, with roughly 3,500 square feet of climbing surface split between training boards and bouldering walls. It also has a mezzanine and fitness equipment, and the article says it should open within a few weeks.
