## Competitions

**American Annie Sanders finished third in the Boulder Series ranking after the Salt Lake City finale** (October 18)

The last World Climbing Series stop of 2026 was held in Salt Lake City, USA. Erin McNeice of Great Britain won the women's series ranking with 4545 points, and Sanders placed third with 3755. Sanders was 7th in the final, and Oriane Bertone of France won the final with 84.6 points.

[World Climbing](https://www.worldclimbing.com/events/world-climbing-series-salt-lake-city-2026/news/bertone-back-on-top)

**Yonatan Katz of Israel won the men's boulder final in Salt Lake City** (October 17)

Katz topped the last boulder on his fourth attempt in only his second senior World Climbing Series event, scoring 69.7 points. Sorato Anraku of Japan took the men's series title with 5048.7 points, after winning five of the six golds available in 2026.

## Crags & Access

**Access Fund changes how Joint Membership money is shared with local climbing groups** (October 19)

From November 2, half of net Joint Membership contributions will go to the participating local climbing organizations, replacing fixed per-gift payments. The minimum for a Joint Membership drops from $50 to $5, and the Access Fund t-shirt threshold falls from $100 to $75 a year.

[Access Fund](https://accessfund.org/latest-news/joint-membership-updates)
