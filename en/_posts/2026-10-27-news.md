## Competitions

**American Annie Sanders won the Lead Series ranking without competing in the Santiago finale** (October 26)

The last Lead stop of the season was in Santiago, Chile. Sanders finished first in the women's series ranking with 4,415 points, ahead of Janja Garnbret (3,805) and Seo Chaehyun of Korea (3,565). Another American, Ella Fisher, placed 8th in the women's final.

[World Climbing](https://www.worldclimbing.com/events/world-climbing-series-santiago-2026/news/garnbret-and-utelli-close-the-season-with-gold-in-santiago)

**Garnbret and Utelli took the Lead golds in Santiago** (October 26)

Garnbret topped the women's final route, with Laura Rogora of Italy second on 48+. In the men's final Jonas Utelli of Switzerland won with 52, ahead of Putra Tri Ramadani of Indonesia (49) and Suzuki Neo of Japan (47+). Suzuki took the men's series title with 4,820 points.

[World Climbing](https://www.worldclimbing.com/events/world-climbing-series-santiago-2026/news/garnbret-and-utelli-close-the-season-with-gold-in-santiago)

**World Climbing Europe published its 2027 calendar** (October 27)

The calendar lists 26 events, including youth and senior series, youth championships and a para championship in Geneva. The Netherlands, Norway and Sweden will host World Climbing Europe events for the first time.

[World Climbing](https://www.worldclimbing.com/news/world-climbing-europe-releases-2027-calendar)

## Outdoor Ascents

**20-year-old Cy McIntosh made the first ascent of a 5.14d in Montana** (October 26)

The route, Keep Feeding Ya, climbs about 60 meters of roof to a blue streak, and McIntosh added a hard extension of more than 15 meters above it. He reportedly sent it on his first try on a day off, after projecting it for about two months.

[Gripped](https://gripped.com/news/new-5-14d-climbed-in-montana-by-cy-mcintosh/)

**Adam Ondra climbed a 5.13 crack on a stadium in Innsbruck, Austria** (October 26)

Ondra sent an urban concrete line first climbed by David Lama and Jorg Verhoeven about 20 years ago. Pete Whittaker tried it too, then the pair made a first ascent on a bridge to end the session.
