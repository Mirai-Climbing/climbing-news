Climbing news from public sources, gathered and summarized by AI every morning (US Eastern time). The English edition focuses on the United States first, then the rest of the world.

We cover competitions, crags (access, closures, rebolting), gym openings and closings, outdoor ascents, gear, and events. Each item links to the original article. This is a prototype run by the makers of the Mirai Climbing app; corrections and tips are welcome on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mirai-Climbing/climbing-news/issues).
